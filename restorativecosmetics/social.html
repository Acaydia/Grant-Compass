Post 9 — Kintsugi (Brand Philosophy)
Kintsugi · 金継ぎ
"The mend itself becomes the most beautiful part."
Restorative Cosmetics · restorativecosmetics.com
Caption — Kintsugi

In Japan, there's an art form called kintsugi — the practice of repairing broken pottery with gold, so that the break itself becomes the most beautiful part of the piece.

That's the philosophy at the heart of everything I do.

Not hiding. Not erasing. Restoring — with skill, with care, with gold.
