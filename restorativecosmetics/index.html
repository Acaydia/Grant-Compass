01 — The House

A quarter-century of restoring what was thought unrecoverable.

Restorative Cosmetics is the paramedical practice of Bethani, a Softap-trained master artist who has spent twenty-five years training technicians around the world in the discipline of restorative pigmentation.

After a decade dedicated to building the next generation of the craft, she is returning to the chair — bringing with her a body of knowledge few in this field possess.

The work is precise, clinical, and quietly transformative: scar camouflage, stretch mark camouflage, areola restoration, vitiligo camouflage, and scalp micropigmentation.

"Restoration is not about hiding what happened. It's about giving skin its dignity back — with the same care a master restorer gives a work of art."

Every client arrives having lived with something they were told was permanent. Every session is built on the belief that it does not have to stay that way — and that the process of restoring it can itself be a form of art.

Restorative Disciplines

Each discipline is built on precision, color mastery, and a clinical understanding of skin — performed with the unhurried care that genuine restoration demands.

I.
Scar Camouflage
Precision pigment work that softens and blends surgical, traumatic, and self-harm scarring into the surrounding skin tone.
II.
Stretch Mark Camouflage
Restorative pigmentation that reduces the visibility of striae, returning skin to a more even, confident canvas.
III.
Areola Restoration
3D areola-complex pigmentation for post-mastectomy and reconstructive clients, restored with anatomical artistry.
IV.
Vitiligo Camouflage
Careful color-matching that restores visual uniformity to depigmented skin with a natural, undetectable finish.
V.
Scalp Micropigmentation
Hairline and density illusion through meticulous follicle-simulation pigmentation for thinning or scarred scalps.
VI.
Practitioner Training
Twenty-five years of technique, standards, and business craft — passed to the next generation of solo artists.

The Artist

The craft, returned to its source.

Bethani Broussard is a Softap-certified master artist with twenty-five years in paramedical cosmetic artistry. She has trained technicians across the globe, establishing the standards by which the next generation works.

After a decade devoted to education, she is returning to the chair — bringing with her a clinical eye, a refined color sensibility, and a philosophy rooted in the belief that restoration is among the most meaningful acts in the craft.

She works with each client as a collaborator — unhurried, precise, and deeply committed to results that feel like yours.
